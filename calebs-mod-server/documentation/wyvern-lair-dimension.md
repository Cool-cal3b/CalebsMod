# The Wyvern Lair

Back to the [all-worlds index](all-worlds.md).

The Wyvern Lair is the additional world supplied by **Mo' Creatures: Nostalgia
Edition 1.0.0**. It is a floating-island dimension focused on wyverns and the
resources associated with them.

## What is there?

The installed dimension generator includes four environment types:

- Wyvern Lair lands
- Wyvern Lair forests
- Wyvern Lair mountains
- Wyvern Lair desert lands

The cache also confirms Wyvern Nest blocks and local iron, gold, lapis,
emerald, and diamond ores. Mo' Creatures contains many wyvern variants,
including jungle, swamp, savanna, sand, mother, undead, light, dark, arctic,
cave, mountain, and sea wyverns.

## Entering

The access item is the **Wyvern Portal Staff**. Either installed recipe uses a
Blaze Rod and an Eye of Ender, with the middle ingredient being either Essence
of Light or a Unicorn Horn.

```text
Empty       Empty                Eye of Ender
Empty       Essence of Light     Empty
Blaze Rod   Empty                Empty
```

The alternate recipe replaces Essence of Light with a Unicorn Horn.

## Travel advice

The terrain consists of floating islands, so bring blocks, food, ranged
weapons, and protection from falling. Do not assume every wyvern is safe to
approach. Prepare a secure arrival point before concentrating on nests or ore.

The installed dimension ID is `mocreatures:wyvernlairworld`.
