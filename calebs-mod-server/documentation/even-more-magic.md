# Even More Magic

Even More Magic is a large loot-and-progression mod built around elemental scrolls, upgradeable wands, Curio trinkets, and magical workstations. This guide covers the useful parts without listing every spell or artifact.

This information was verified against the installed **Even More Magic 1.869 for Minecraft 1.20.1** files. Use EMI and search `@even_more_magic` for the exact recipe of a specific item.

## What to keep from ruins

The mod adds scroll ruins, wizard houses, towers, temples, fountains, and other structures. Their chests are the main source of the mod's progression items.

- **Magical Scroll Bundle:** Right-click to consume it and receive a set of magical scrolls. Keep at least one of each useful scroll so it can be copied later.
- **Enchantinuuxxreenatiuurutunium Powder:** The long-name powder is the mod's main crafting currency. It is needed for wands, scroll copying, rings, and workstations.
- **Columns:** Sandstone, cobblestone, brick, red sandstone, redstone, prismarine, packed ice, and powder columns all work in the same crafting ring. Collect nine total. They can be mixed.
- **Wand upgrades and Ring Synthesizer Upgrade:** These are valuable progression pieces. The Ring Synthesizer Upgrade is found in End ancient forge ruins.
- **Curio trinkets:** Check the colored rarity and stat tooltip before recycling one. The order is generally Core, Rare, Epic, then Omega.

## Build a column ring

The **Sandstone Column** and the other column variants are magical crafting pedestals. Build nine columns on the same level in this pattern. `C` is a column and the center `X` is also a column.

```text
...C...
.C...C.
.......
C..X..C
.......
.C...C.
...C...
```

The four straight outer columns are three blocks from the center. The four diagonal columns are two blocks away in both directions.

Right-click columns to place recipe ingredients on them. Most progression recipes put the main item on the center column, one ingredient on each of the eight outer columns, then use an activation item on the center. EMI shows the correct column recipe and ingredients.

Important wand steps:

1. Craft a basic Core wand such as an Emerald, Diamond, or Amethyst Wand in a crafting table.
2. To make many Rare wands, place the Core wand in the center and eight matching scrolls around it, then activate the center with the long-name powder.
3. Epic upgrades usually use a Rare wand in the center, eight powder around it, and an elemental wand upgrade as the activation item.
4. Omega upgrades usually use an Epic wand in the center, eight powder blocks around it, and an Omega Wand Upgrade.

Do not guess expensive column recipes. Check the output in EMI first.

## Best things to build

| Build | Why it matters | Main materials |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scroll Multiplier** | Copies a scroll so rare spells are not consumed by wand recipes. Each copy uses the original scroll with one paper, one stick, and one long-name powder. | Powder, glass, diamond, amethyst shards, and cobbled deepslate |
| **Ring Synthesizer** | Combines two magical rings into a stronger ring that covers more elements. | Diamond Essence, Iron Essence, an anvil, and powder blocks |
| **Ring Forge** | Upgraded Ring Synthesizer that can add a third ring to upgraded rings. Build it through the column ring with the Ring Synthesizer in the center, eight Diamond Essence around it, and the Ring Synthesizer Upgrade as the activator. | Ring Synthesizer, 8 Diamond Essence, and the upgrade |
| **Artifact Recycling Table** | Recycles Core, Rare, Epic, and Omega items back into long-name powder. Sneak and right-click the machine to recycle its contents. | Sculk shriekers, sculk catalysts, and powder |
| **Artifact Recycling Storage** | Place beside the Recycling Table to collect its recovered powder automatically. | Iron Essence, Diamond Essence, sculk catalyst, and powder |

The Scroll Multiplier is usually the best first machine. A single scroll can be copied until there are enough copies for a column-ring wand recipe.

## Wands worth watching for

There are far too many wands to rank completely, but these have especially useful jobs:

| Wand | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Torch Wand / Soul Torch Wand** | Places a torch at the targeted location. The Soul version can switch between regular and soul torches. |
| **Wand of Iron Ore / Iron Vein** | Highlights iron ore locations. |
| **Glowing Wand** | Makes entities near the hit position glow, useful for finding mobs through terrain. |
| **Wand of Treasure Fairy** | Summons a fairy that periodically highlights nearby blocks containing inventories. |
| **Jetpack Wand / Wind Rocket Wand** | Provides vertical flight or a fast forward dash. Both have cooldown limitations. |
| **Wand of Grass Steed** | Summons a temporary rideable grass block. |
| **Wand of Little Rain** | Extinguishes nearby burning entities and fire blocks. |
| **Wand of Obsidian Crusher / Chainsaw** | Cuts obsidian. |
| **Wand of Integrity** | Heals 75 percent of maximum health. |

**Caution:** the Wand of Cave Escape and Wand of Cave Elevator mine upward, but their tooltips state that nearby ores can be replaced with cobblestone or cobbled deepslate. Do not use them in an ore-rich area.

## Trinkets and the furnace backpack

Open the Curios inventory to equip trinkets in their matching slots. Trinkets raise five magic attributes: **Agility, Destruction, Knowledge, Restoration, and Summoning**. Those attributes improve effects described on wand and scroll tooltips. Elemental rings instead reduce cooldown and full-charge time for matching spell elements.

The wearable **Furnace** is a back-slot Curio, not a portable smelting menu. Its family is found in earth-themed structure loot and provides these stats:

| Back item | Tier | Bonus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Furnace | Core | +1 Destruction, +1 Restoration |
| Smoker | Rare | +2 Destruction, +1 Restoration |
| Blast Furnace | Epic | +2 Destruction, +2 Restoration |
| Classic Furnace | Omega | +3 Destruction, +2 Restoration |

Some excellent high-tier targets are:

| Trinket | Slot | Bonus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fallen Star** | Accessory | +2 to all five magic attributes |
| **Edgy Air Jet Engines** | Back | +5 Agility |
| **Dragon Wings** | Feet | +5 Agility |
| **Molten Sky** | Accessory | +5 Destruction |
| **Forest Aura** | Aura | +5 Restoration |
| **Stardust Aura** | Aura | +4 Knowledge |
| **Frozen Heart** | Accessory | +4 Summoning |
| **Life Heart Locket** | Necklace | +4 Restoration |

An item called **Magical Recharger** is also worth saving. Right-click it while holding a wand in the off-hand to restore half of the wand's maximum durability. The wand may activate during the process, so recharge dangerous spells in a safe area.

## Rings in one minute

Rings specialize a spell loadout. Early single-element rings give smaller bonuses, while combined rings cover two or three elements and can reach a 40 percent reduction to cooldown and full-charge time. Use the Ring Synthesizer for two-ring combinations and the Ring Forge for advanced three-ring combinations. The exact pairings are recipe-specific, so inspect the desired output in EMI before spending rings or powder.

## Recommended progression

1. Loot magical structures for Scroll Bundles, powder, nine columns, Curios, and upgrades.
2. Build the column ring and a Scroll Multiplier.
3. Keep one copy of every useful scroll and multiply it for recipes.
4. Make a practical Rare wand first, such as a movement, detection, healing, or torch wand.
5. Build the Ring Synthesizer when enough essences and rings have accumulated.
6. Build the Recycling Table only after learning which trinkets and spell families fit the preferred build.
7. Save End forge loot for the Ring Forge and late-game combinations.
