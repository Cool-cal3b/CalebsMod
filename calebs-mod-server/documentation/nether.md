# The Nether

Back to the [all-worlds index](all-worlds.md).

The Nether is still the vanilla Nether dimension, but this pack substantially
expands its terrain, structures, hazards, resources, and wildlife.

## Added biomes

**Regions Unexplored** adds five confirmed Nether biomes:

- **Blackstone Basin**
- **Glistering Meadow**
- **Infernal Holt**
- **Mycotoxic Undergrowth**
- **Redstone Abyss**

These are full Nether biomes, not separate dimensions.

## Structures and hazards

- **Philip's Ruins** adds Nether lava ruins, Nether ruins, and larger Nether
  dungeon complexes.
- **Doomlike Dungeons** includes a dedicated hard Nether dungeon theme.
- **Structures of Eternia** adds a Nether Joker Tower.
- **Deadly World** can generate TNT, lava, fire, and potion traps, dispenser
  towers, silverfish nests, and several hostile spawner types in Nether
  terrain.

This makes apparently ordinary ruins and isolated mechanisms worth treating
carefully. Some containers and spawners can also turn out to be mimics.

## Resources and unusual destinations

- **Tinkers' Construct** adds **Blood Islands** to Nether lava oceans and
  generates Nether Cobalt Ore.
- **Malum** adds Nether resources including Blazing Quartz.
- **Tide** supports lava fishing with Nether catches such as the Magma
  Mackerel, Ashen Perch, Soulscaler, and Blazing Swordfish.
- **Chococraft** adds Nether Chocobos.
- Mo' Creatures can place Fire Manticores in Nether biome categories.

## What is most worth finding?

For scenery, search for the five Regions Unexplored biomes. For progression,
Blood Islands and cobalt are the most important Tinkers' Construct finds. For
combat and loot, Philip's Ruins and Doomlike Dungeons offer the largest
destinations, while Deadly World makes even small discoveries hazardous.

New terrain additions are most reliably found in unexplored chunks.
