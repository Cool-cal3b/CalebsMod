# The Overworld

Back to the [all-worlds index](all-worlds.md).

The Overworld is the server's main building and exploration world. It also
contains the ordinary routes into most other dimensions.

## Major exploration additions

- **Regions Unexplored**, **Traverse**, **Terrestria**, and **Wilder Wilds**
  broaden the terrain, vegetation, and biome variety.
- **Dungeon Crawl**, **Doomlike Dungeons**, **Philip's Ruins**, **ATI
  Structures**, **Keebsz's Battle Towers**, and other structure mods add many
  reasons to explore above and below ground.
- **Deadly World** can hide traps, dangerous spawners, and mimics among normal
  exploration rewards.
- The pack contains many additional animals, monsters, treasures, equipment
  systems, and magic systems. This page is a world overview rather than a full
  catalogue of every Overworld mod.

For known enemy behavior, see the [common hostile mobs guide](common-hostile-mobs.md).

## Gateways from the Overworld

- [Aerial Hell](aerial-hell-dimension.md) uses a Stellar Portal. The complete
  construction instructions are in the [Stellar Portal guide](stellar-portal.md).
- [Twilight Forest](twilight-forest-dimension.md) uses its characteristic
  water-pool portal.
- [Wyvern Lair](wyvern-lair-dimension.md) is reached with the Wyvern Portal
  Staff.
- [Cave Stuff](cave-stuff-dimension.md) includes a Testing Portal Igniter for
  its cave dimension.
- [DimensionalDoors](dimensional-doors-guide.md) entrances lead to
  pockets, dungeons, and potentially Limbo.
- Vanilla portals still lead to the [Nether](nether.md) and [End](end.md).
