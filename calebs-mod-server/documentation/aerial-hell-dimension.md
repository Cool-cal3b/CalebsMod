# The Stellar Dimension (Aerial Hell)

What you'll find once you're actually through a Stellar Portal. For how to
build and light the portal itself, see the Stellar Portal guide. All of this
comes straight out of `aerialhell-0.7.0.1_forge1.20.1.jar`.

Aerial Hell is a floating sky world, no ceiling, lots of open air between
islands. It has its own stone (Stellar Stone) and its own ore list, none of
which generate back in the Overworld.

## The gems and what they're actually for

| Material | Found | How rare | Ore height | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Fluorite** | Stellar Stone, everywhere | most common | Y 20 to 256 | Torches, lanterns, blocks, green dye, the Oscillator, the Stellar Lighter |
| **Ruby** | Stellar Stone | common | Y 20 to 256 | Full tool/armor set, Ruby Blowpipe, Stellar Lighter |
| **Azurite** | Stellar Stone | uncommon | Y 20 to 170 | Azurite Crystal (crafting/decorative) |
| **Volucite** | Stellar Stone, high up only | rare | Y 160 to 256 | End game tool/armor set, Blowpipe |

Fluorite and Azurite are safe to smelt in a normal furnace, no downside.

**Ruby and Volucite are not.** Smelt Ruby Ore in a regular furnace and you get
"Overheated Ruby," a useless dead item you can't do anything with except fix
it. Same story for Volucite. The only way to get the real gem is to run the
ore (or the overheated/raw version) through an **Oscillator**.

## The Oscillator

Crafted from 8 Stellar Stone around 1 Fluorite. It's Aerial Hell's answer to
a furnace, a block that "vibrates" items instead of burning them. Anything
that needs oscillating just doesn't work in a normal furnace, or gives a
worse result.

What it processes:

- **Ore into real gems:** Ruby, Volucite, Azurite, and the Stellar Stone
  Crystal all go through it. Ruby and Volucite specifically require it.
- **Stone conversion:** turns Stellar Cobblestone into Stellar Stone, and
  regular Clay into Stellar Clay.
- **Food upgrades:** cooking food in the Oscillator gives a "Vibrant" version
  of it (steak, phantom meat, turtle meat, chicken, berries, soup, etc), all
  better than the plain cooked version.
- **Misc:** rotten leather back into usable leather, Slippery Sand into glass.

Since it works like a furnace, it needs fuel. Keep coal or similar on hand.

## The Stellar Furnace

Not the same thing as the Oscillator. The Stellar Furnace is just a regular
furnace made out of Stellar Cobblestone instead of vanilla cobblestone, for
when you're stuck in Aerial Hell without Overworld stone. It only runs
ordinary smelting recipes, same as a normal furnace, so Ruby and Volucite
ore still come out ruined ("Overheated") if you use it. It exists purely so
you have a furnace at all; it doesn't replace the Oscillator.

There's also a **Freezer**, the Oscillator's cold counterpart: it turns
lava buckets into Crystal Blocks, water buckets into ice, the "Liquid of
the Gods" buckets into obsidian, Azurite Crystal into vanilla quartz, and
Volucite Vibrant into an actual vanilla diamond. It also makes "Frozen"
versions of food, the cold equivalent of the Oscillator's "Vibrant" foods.

## What "Crystal" is actually for

If you've picked some up and wondered why it doesn't craft into gear: it
doesn't. Plain **Crystal** (from oscillating a Stellar Stone Crystal, or
dropped by Crystal Slimes/Spiders/Golems/Caterpillars and the Lunar Priest)
only crafts into a Crystal Block, which in turn makes Crystal Bricks. That's
the whole chain, it's a decorative/building material in this version, not a
gear material. Don't hoard it expecting a tool set.

## Wildlife

Mostly harmless and mostly food/materials:

- **Stellar Chicken, Stellar Boar, Sandy Sheep, Gliding Turtle, Kodama**:
  passive, farm them like their vanilla equivalents.
- **Forest Caterpillar**: passive bug, turns hostile as a Crystal Caterpillar
  variant in crystal biomes.
- **Worm**: hostile snake-like mob that targets players. Avoid its melee attack.

## Bad guys to watch for

General hostiles that roam the dimension:

- **Stellar Ent**: the one that probably jumped you. Spawns in every single
  Aerial Hell biome, packs of 4 to 10, one of the most common hostiles in
  the dimension. Drops are mundane though, just Sky Bowl and Sky Stick
  (the local stand-ins for vanilla stick/bowl, used in tons of basic
  recipes), nothing dangerous to lose.
- **Automaton / Stellar Stone Automaton**: animated stone constructs.
- **Verdigris Zombie, Mummy**: zombie-type mobs; Mummies cluster around the
  Upside Down Pyramid along with **Venomous Snakes**.
- **Crystal Slime, Crystal Spider, Crystal Golem, Crystal Caterpillar**:
  found in the crystal biomes.
- **Evil Cow / Cortinarius Cow**: hostile cattle. Cortinarius Cows are the
  mushroom-forest variant, found in Cortinarius Violaceus, Giant Red Mushroom,
  and Verdigris Agaric forests. Don't milk either.
- **Shroomboom**: creeper-like mushroom monster in those same mushroom
  forests. Give it plenty of room.
- **Hell Spider**: hostile spider found in the Slippery Sand Ocean.
- **Torn Spirit, Ice Spirit, Fire Spirit, Electro Spirit**: flying casters,
  Torn Spirits guard the Golden Nether Prison in packs of 3 to 5.
- **Fat Phantom, Flying Jellyfish**: airborne nuisances.
- **Slime Pirates / Ghost Slime Pirates**: found around the floating boat
  wrecks.

Shadow biomes (Shadow Forest, Shadow Plain) are their own tier of danger:
**Shadow Troll, Shadow Automaton, Shadow Spider**, and the occasional
**Shadow Flying Skull**. Killing any of them can drop a **Shadow Shard**,
the crafting material for Cursed gear, so shadow biomes are worth farming
even outside the Shadow Catacombs.

## Major structures and their bosses

Four proper dungeons, each with a boss that drops the raw material for its
own full gear line. The boss materials are craftable into complete
tool/weapon/armor sets, and the dungeon's own chests can also drop
pre-made (sometimes pre-enchanted) pieces of the same set.

| Structure | Where | Guarded by | Boss | Boss drops | Gear line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Golden Nether Prison** | any Aerial Hell biome, Y 90 to 155 | Torn Spirits (3 to 5) | **Chained God** | Arsonist Ingot | Arsonist set (stronger while you're on fire), God Sword, Netherian King Sword, Berserk Axe |
| **Shadow Catacombs** | Shadow biomes only, underground, Y 35 to 55 | Shadow Trolls, Shadow Automatons, Shadow Spiders (packs) | **Lilith** | Shadow Shard (chest loot) | Shadow/Cursed set, Reaper Scythe |
| **Lunar Temple** (Lunatic Temple) | non-shadow Aerial Hell biomes, Y 135 to 190 | Crystal Spiders (5 to 6) | **Lunar Priest** | Lunatic Crystal | Lunatic set, Sword/Axe of Light (shoot a Lunar Projectile) |
| **Mud Dungeon** | any Aerial Hell biome, Y 20 to 50 | Mud Soldiers (5 to 15) | **Mud Cycle Mage** | Obsidian Shard | Obsidian set (slow, powerful Heavy Sword/Axe) |

**Mud Soldiers** are not limited to the dungeon. They also spawn naturally in
the Slippery Sand Ocean, Shadow Forest, and Shadow Plain.

**Upside Down Pyramid** (desert-like biome, sky level) has no boss, just
Mummies and Venomous Snakes guarding a chest full of Ruby gear. Good early
stop once you've got a lighter.

There's also a **Stellar Stone Bricks Tower** and a **Shadow Pine Tower**
scattered around; smaller loot structures, not dungeons.

**Azurite in one line:** it mines and hits exactly like Gold, meaning it
*cannot* mine iron, diamond, redstone, or lapis ore, obsidian, or ancient
debris (mining level 0), and its attack damage bonus is 0. What it's good
for is durability (better than Gold) and enchanting, its 22 enchantability
ties Gold for the best in the pack. Good enchanting-fodder or beginner tools
before you get to Ruby, bad for actually mining Aerial Hell's ore veins.

### Armor

Protection is boots/leggings/chestplate/helmet. Only these materials have a
full armor set, Sky Wood, Stellar Stone, Breaker, and Heavy don't.

If you're optimizing for pure numbers: **Arsonist** is the best all-around
armor in the pack (highest protection, full Netherite toughness and
knockback resistance, plus the fire perk). **Volucite** is a close second on
protection and its flight ability makes it worth it anyway.

## Worth knowing

- **Volucite armor lets you fly** (full set grants "Volucite Power," on top
  of the passive Slow Falling from just carrying Volucite Vibrant).
- **Magmatic Gel** (used for Magma Cube tools that give Jump Boost) turns up
  around the Mud Dungeon area, worth grabbing while you're down there.
- Cursed weapons (Cursed Sword/Axe) hit hard but reflect part of the damage
  back at you, don't go in with low health.
