# The End

Back to the [all-worlds index](all-worlds.md).

The End retains its vanilla biome layout in this pack. Regions Unexplored does
not add End biomes, but several installed mods add islands, structures,
features, creatures, and fishing content.

## Major destinations

- **Tinkers' Construct** adds **Enderslime Islands** to the outer End, along
  with Ender geodes and End slimes.
- **Philip's Ruins** adds End ruins and an **End Gate Fortress**.
- **Tide** adds End oases, which create pockets of water and support End
  fishing.
- **Even More Magic** adds Ancient Forge ruins in the End.
- **Structures of Eternia** adds an End Joker Tower.

## Fishing and resources

Tide's End content includes End loot crates and catches such as the **End
Stone Perch**, **Purpur Pike**, and rare **Voidseeker**. End oases are the most
obvious places to investigate when looking for this content.

## What is most worth finding?

Enderslime Islands are the largest departure from vanilla terrain and are an
important Tinkers' Construct destination. The End Gate Fortress and End ruins
provide the strongest conventional exploration targets. Oases make the End
feel less empty and introduce a separate fishing activity.

Most of these additions target the outer End, so reaching fresh islands after
defeating the Ender Dragon is the practical starting point. New features are
most reliably found in unexplored chunks.
