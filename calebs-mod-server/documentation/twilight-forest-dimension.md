# The Twilight Forest

Back to the [all-worlds index](all-worlds.md).

The server includes **Twilight Forest 4.3.2508**. It is a large, continuous
adventure dimension built around landmarks, boss encounters, and enforced
progression.

## Why visit?

Twilight Forest is the strongest choice for a guided group adventure. Its
biomes contain major structures associated with bosses such as the Naga,
Lich, Minoshroom, Hydra, Knight Phantom, Ur-Ghast, Alpha Yeti, and Snow Queen.
The active mod includes progression rules that can lock later biomes until the
required earlier encounters are completed.

## Entering

The familiar Twilight Forest portal is a small water pool surrounded by
natural vegetation. Activating it opens the route to the dimension. Recipe
viewers and the Twilight Forest advancements are useful for checking the
current progression order once inside.

## Travel advice

- Treat each large landmark as part of a progression chain rather than an
  isolated loot structure.
- Keep trophies and unusual boss drops. They can be evidence of progression
  and may be useful later.
- Bring a map, food, spare equipment, and a reliable way to mark routes.
- Travel together when entering a new locked biome or structure.

Twilight Forest terrain is stored as `twilightforest:twilight_forest` in the
current server world.
