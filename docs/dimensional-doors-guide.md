# DimensionalDoors guide

This server includes **DimensionalDoors 5.4.4**. It creates rifts, doors, pocket dungeons, and the Limbo dimension. A doorway found in the world can lead to a generated dungeon made of connected rooms, including laboratory-style rooms and mazes.

## Is this a real dimension?

Yes, but it works differently from the Nether or Twilight Forest. DimensionalDoors registers several separate dimensions for dungeon pockets, public pockets, personal pockets, and Limbo. Pocket dungeons are generated as small, isolated spaces connected by rifts rather than one continuous open world to explore. Limbo is the larger hostile escape dimension.

## Before entering a rift

Treat the entrance as your route home. Do not break the door or the rift after going through it, and do not rely on an untested exit. If the entrance rift closes, the mod's own messages say that trying to leave the pocket sends the player to Limbo instead.

Bring food, blocks, a weapon, light, and a way to mark your route. Travel with a friend when possible, and keep valuable gear at home until you understand the dungeon.

## The black void left at the entrance

The black shape is an exposed **rift**. On this server, rift growth and rift decay are enabled, so it can grow and damage nearby blocks. It is not harmless visual debris.

There are two intended remedies:

- **Rift Stabilizer:** use it on the rift's core to stop the rift from growing. It preserves the rift.
- **Rift Remover:** use it near an exposed rift to close it and nearby rifts. This destroys the link, so make certain nobody still needs to exit through it.

If no appropriate tool is available, keep people away from the area and ask an administrator for help. Do not try to fix it by breaking more blocks or by entering the growing rift.

## Loot worth looking for

The bundled DimensionalDoors dungeon-chest table can roll 2 to 6 regular items plus a small chance at an enchanted book. Its best confirmed rewards are:

- Enchanted books with treasure enchantments, at level 30, though this roll is uncommon.
- Golden apples, which are very rare.
- Diamonds, 1 to 2 at a time.
- World Thread, 2 to 12 at a time. It is a DimensionalDoors crafting material and is used for Woven World Thread armor.

Iron, gold, emeralds, coal, quartz, books, and lime fabric can also appear. Other structures in the modpack may add loot through their own tables, so this is the confirmed DimensionalDoors chest loot, not a promise for every chest found inside a dungeon.

Killing a Mask can yield a Mask Shard. The cache also includes rift-related tools and higher-tier materials, but their loot sources are not confirmed by the installed dungeon-chest table.

## Threats and things to know

- **Getting stranded:** a closed entrance can send a player to Limbo when they attempt to leave the pocket.
- **Rifts:** exposed rifts can grow and decay the surrounding area under this server's active settings. Detached rifts also have a very small configured chance to spawn Endermen. Half of those spawned Endermen are configured to become aggressive.
- **Monoliths:** the mod includes Monoliths, especially relevant in Limbo. On this server they are not configured to attack and deal damage, but their gaze teleportation is enabled. Looking at one for too long can teleport a player to the void above Limbo.
- **Masks:** Mask is the other dedicated DimensionalDoors mob confirmed in the installed files. Player kills can drop Mask Shards.
- **Dungeon hazards:** expect traps, dead ends, vertical drops, and normal hostile mobs where a structure provides spawning space. Do not assume every door is an exit.

## Limbo and escaping it

Limbo is the failure-state dimension for this mod. The server is **not** configured to send players there for ordinary deaths outside pocket dimensions, and Hardcore Limbo is disabled. The mod's text identifies finding an exit rift, using Eternal Fluid, or dying as ways to escape in applicable situations. The server is set to return escapees to the Overworld, normally around world spawn rather than their bed.

## Bosses

No dedicated DimensionalDoors boss is confirmed in this installed cache. Monoliths are a special environmental threat, not a conventional boss fight. Other mods in the pack can still place their own hostile mobs or bosses in structures, so a dungeon can be dangerous without DimensionalDoors itself providing a boss.

## Quick rules

1. Leave the way in intact until everyone is out.
2. If a rift appears, stabilize it to preserve it or remove it only after it is no longer needed.
3. Avoid staring at Monoliths in Limbo.
4. Loot quickly, mark the route, and do not enter a new door without an exit plan.
